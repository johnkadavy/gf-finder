# Restaurant outreach — copy bank

Copy for the pilot invite email (PRIORITIES.md item A). The Tally form intro is
deliberately short; the business case lives here, in the email.

## Business case block (email)

```text
Why gluten-free diners matter
• More than 1 in 10 Americans limit or avoid gluten — for celiac disease, gluten sensitivity, or by choice. In New York, that's hundreds of thousands of potential diners.
• For a celiac diner, one mistake means days of illness, so they research before they go. When they find a place they trust, they come back, and they bring friends and family.
• One celiac diner often decides where the whole table eats.
• More and more, that research starts with Google and AI answers, and CleanPlate is one of the sources those answers draw on.
```

## Email structure (planned)

1. Personalized opener: their score and the main reason it isn't higher ("here's why you're at a 62").
2. What CleanPlate is (1–2 sentences).
3. Business case block above.
4. Their listing link. Later: Search Console appearances for their page, and a screenshot of what the AI Overview says about them.
5. The ask: the invite link to the form (~8–10 min, free, can raise or lower the score).

Keep the verified-listing offer out of the email and the protocol form. It's pitched only on the second form, after the kitchen-practices answers are submitted.

## Stats — sources and caveats

- **"More than 1 in 10"**: CivicScience (2022) — 4% of US adults fully gluten-free for medical reasons, another 10% limit gluten for medical reasons; diet-by-choice is on top. https://civicscience.com/?p=36645
- **"Hundreds of thousands" in NYC**: our own estimate — even 4% of ~8.3M New Yorkers ≈ 330k.
- **Rejected:** "1 in 5" (IFIC/Gallup 2013–15, peak GF-fad era, overstates); "$12.6B lost" and "95% abandoned plans" (vendor-commissioned Nutritics survey); "44% of non-allergic diners" (no traceable source); "up to 24% higher profitability" (secondhand slides).
- Celiac prevalence if ever needed: ~0.7–1% of Americans (NHANES 2009–10), mostly undiagnosed. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/22850429/

## Verified listing offer (second form) — decided so far

- Founding-member price, collected as interest only (no charge before a lawyer's read). Rotate $99 / $149 / $249 per year by hand; record the price shown (`price_variant`).
- Annual hosted visit: announced, meal paid for by the restaurant, photos + honest dish write-up, labeled "meal provided by the restaurant". Presentation only, never moves the score.
- Score-affecting verification is separate: unannounced, paid by CleanPlate, can come back negative.
